    Mechanics

    How an epoch settles

    STEP 1

    Claim fees

    The keeper claims PFX's pump.fun creator fees in SOL. A small reserve stays back for gas and token-account rent.

    STEP 2

    Screen and pick

    About 100 top pump.fun coins are screened. Outliers are hidden and the top five survivors by market cap form the basket.

    STEP 3

    Buy the basket

    SOL is split by weight and market-bought through Jupiter. It routes across PumpSwap, Raydium and Meteora.

    STEP 4

    Airdrop

    The final snapshot is compared with every snapshot taken during the epoch. Wallets that never dipped get the five tokens pro rata.
